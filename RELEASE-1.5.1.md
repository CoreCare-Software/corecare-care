# CoreCare Enterprise 1.5.1 — Integrated Rota & Live Visits

## Included
- Rota planning page backed by the live visit record
- Published rota entries automatically appear in electronic call monitoring
- Weekly recurrence generation
- Staff allocation and unallocated warnings
- Staff overlap protection
- Live scheduled, late, in-progress, overrunning and completed status
- Rota cancellation linked to the live visit
- Client and staff filters
- Audit events for publish, update and cancellation
- Existing offline electronic call monitoring retained

## Deployment
Database migration required: Yes (`0023_integrated_rota_live_visits.sql`).

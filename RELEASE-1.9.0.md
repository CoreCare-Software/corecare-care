# CoreCare Enterprise 1.9.0 — Adaptive Allocation Board

- Moves unallocated visits above the care-worker rota on the same shared timeline.
- Automatically expands the allocation area as simultaneous and total unallocated visits increase.
- Adds a draggable, keyboard-accessible splitter with locally remembered height.
- Stacks overlapping unallocated visits into separate timeline lanes.
- Allows visits to be dragged between the allocation timeline and care-worker rows, including time changes.
- Adds visit-type colours and staff utilisation indicators.
- Keeps allocation and worker timelines horizontally synchronised.

Database migration required: No.

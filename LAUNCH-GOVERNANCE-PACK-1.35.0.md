# CoreCare Care 1.35.0 launch-governance evidence pack

Use this pack to prepare the evidence referenced in **Settings → Launch governance**. Store sensitive supporting records in an approved secure location and enter only the reference and concise evidence summary in the governance workspace.

## 1. Accountable provider

Record the responsible provider, registered manager, release owner and operational system owner. Include the approved role matrix, branch model, enabled-module review, family identity and authority procedure, and the provider’s regulatory decision route.

## 2. Clinical safety

Maintain a provider-owned clinical hazard log with hazard, cause, affected person, existing control, verification evidence, residual likelihood and severity, clinical owner, action and review date. Review care-plan approval, eMAR and PRN rules, controlled-medicine witnessing, body-map escalation, safeguarding, duty of candour and incident closure against current policies.

## 3. Data protection

Reference the approved DPIA, privacy notices, Article 6 and Article 9 conditions where applicable, consent or authority procedures, retention and deletion schedule, subject-rights process, breach route, processor agreements, supplier register and international-transfer position.

## 4. Backup and restore

Record the recovery owner, recovery-time target, recovery-point target, D1 restore rehearsal, Worker rollback rehearsal, test timestamp, isolated destination, result, data-integrity checks, corrective actions and next test date. Do not copy production care data into an unmanaged local file.

## 5. Business continuity

Cover Worker, D1, identity, internet, device and power outages. Include safe paper or offline visit and medication records, current contact lists, escalation thresholds, communication templates, controlled transcription, duplicate prevention, reconciliation, record destruction and return-to-service authority.

## 6. Staff training and competence

Maintain a role matrix for managers, coordinators, senior carers, carers, auditors and family-support staff. Witness scenarios for sign-in, rotas, visits, care plans, eMAR, incidents, family access, reports and outage reconciliation. Record competence outcome, assessor, restrictions, remedial action and refresher date.

## 7. Incident response

Publish technical, clinical, safeguarding, information-governance and out-of-hours contacts. Define severity, evidence preservation, containment, communication, regulatory notification, recovery and post-incident learning. Retain the latest tabletop exercise and actions.

## 8. Production acceptance

Retain the signed UAT script and defects decision. Cover identity and sessions, branch isolation, least privilege, care plans, eMAR, visits, body maps, incidents, documents, family restrictions, finance and reporting exports, monitoring and recovery. The accountable release owner must make the final go/no-go decision after all prerequisite domains are approved.

## Suggested evidence reference format

Use a stable identifier such as `CLIN-SAFE-2026-01`, `DPIA-CARE-2026-02`, `BCP-TEST-2026-03` or `UAT-CARE-1.35.0`. Avoid putting passwords, witness credentials, full clinical narratives or sensitive personal data into the summary field.

# CoreCare Enterprise 1.10.0 — Automated Client Onboarding

## Automated care pathway

Creating a client now includes structured recurring visit requirements. CoreCare automatically creates eight weeks of draft, unallocated visits on the shared scheduling timeline.

The same action creates onboarding checklist records, manager tasks and an in-app warning covering assessment, care plan, risks, medication, consent, funding and visit confirmation.

## Planner control

Generated visits remain editable by authorised planners and managers. The backend supports changes to one visit, future occurrences or the entire recurring series, with a mandatory reason and full change history.

Manual changes remain draft until published. One-off visits remain available for genuine exceptions rather than routine scheduling.

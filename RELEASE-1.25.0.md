# CoreCare Enterprise 1.25.0 — eMAR Operations

## Database migration
Required: `0037_emar_operations.sql`

## Included
- Daily client MAR chart by date and scheduled time.
- Due/outstanding dose display and missed-dose visibility.
- Regular and PRN administration recording.
- PRN reason and effectiveness fields.
- Automatic stock reduction following administration.
- Stock deliveries, returns, waste and audited balance corrections.
- Configurable low-stock thresholds and warnings.
- Audited MAR correction workflow; original entries remain visible and voided.
- Medication discontinuation reason/history fields.
- Printable daily MAR view.
- Medication register, stock, daily chart and history tabs.
